AOSP Internals

The course Inside Android follows the structure of the book AOSP Internals: A Developer's Guide to the Android Open Source Project by the aospbooks authors. You can read the original book online at aospbooks.github.io/aosp-internal-book.

The original book uses the Apache License 2.0. The chapters on this site are new text in simple words, with new diagrams. They are not copies of the original text. Each chapter has a link to the matching chapter in the original book. If a chapter here uses a part of the original book directly, the chapter tells you.

Thank you to the authors of AOSP Internals for an open and very detailed book.

Simplified Technical English

The courses and articles use the writing rules of ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English. The rules make the text clear for all readers, also for readers who do not speak English as a first language. To check the text, I use the simplified-technical-english tool (MIT license). The STE dictionary is the property of ASD.
